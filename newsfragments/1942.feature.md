`UFixed::parse` and `Fixed::parse` read decimal text such as `0.25` or `-1.5`, with at most the type's number of fraction digits: `Wad::parse("0.3")`. `UFixed` and `Fixed` implement `std::io::Display`, writing the shortest exact text without trailing zeros, so `format!(Wad::from_ratio(5, 2))` is `2.5`.
