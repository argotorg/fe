The `mut` part of a product parameter, such as `s.1` for `s: Pair::Step<mut>` with `shape Step = (own u256, mut? u256)`, can now be assigned (`s.1 = v`, `s.1 += v`) and passed as `mut s.1`, as destructuring with `let (_, mut b) = s` already allowed. These were rejected with 8-0018 and 8-0066. Owned and `ref` parts stay read-only, and the error now names them instead of suggesting `var s`.
