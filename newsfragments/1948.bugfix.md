`StoragePackedArray` no longer writes outside the array. Word `w` of the array at slot `s` now lies at `keccak256(w ++ s)`, the entry `w` of a `StorageMap<u256, u256>` at `s`, as in Solady's `LibMap` and OpenZeppelin's `BitMaps`. It used to lie at `keccak256(s) + w`, so a large index wrapped around the slot space and could overwrite another contract field or a map's entries. This changes the array's storage layout: values written by an earlier release are not found at their new slots.
