Added `core::fixed` with decimal fixed-point numbers whose scale is part of the type: `UFixed<D>` and `Fixed<D>` (signed), with the aliases `Wad` (18 decimals), `Ray` (27) and `SWad` (signed 18). `+`, `-`, `*`, `/` and `**` (integer powers) keep the scale; products and quotients use a 512-bit intermediate and round down, `mul_up` and `div_up` round up, and `rescale<E>()` converts between scales explicitly, so `Wad * Ray` or `wad + 5` is a type error. `sqrt` is exact for any scale, and the 18-decimal types port Solady's `expWad`, `lnWad` and `powWad` bit for bit as `SWad::exp`, `Wad::ln` and `Wad::powf`. Constructors and arithmetic are `const fn`, so constants such as `const FEE: Wad = Wad::from_ratio(3, 1000)` are computed at compile time.
