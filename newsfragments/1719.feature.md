Added typed ETH amounts and block time to `std::evm::units`, all in the std prelude: `Wei` (an amount of native ETH), `Timestamp` (unix seconds) and `Duration` (seconds). Amounts, points in time and durations no longer mix: `Timestamp + Duration` is a `Timestamp`, `Timestamp - Timestamp` is a `Duration`, `Wei / Wei` is a plain ratio, and `Timestamp + Timestamp` or `Wei + Duration` do not compile. `Wei` and `Duration` also add and subtract, scale and split by a `u256` (`price * quantity`, `pot / winners`), take a remainder (`value % gwei(1)`) and order, and `Wei::mul_div` computes fees and shares with a 512-bit intermediate. Solidity-style constructors read like unit literals: `ether(5)`, `gwei(30)`, `wei(1)`, `days(7)`, `hours(1)`, along with the constants `WEI`, `GWEI`, `ETHER`, `SECOND`, `MINUTE`, `HOUR`, `DAY` and `WEEK`. They are `const fn`s and work in constant initializers, e.g. `const LOCK: Duration = weeks(2) + days(3)`. `as_wei()` and `as_seconds()` return the plain number. All three types are a single `uint256` in the ABI, so they can be `msg` fields and return values, `#[event]` fields (indexed or not), contract fields and `StorageMap` keys or values.
