`std::io`'s character I/O is gone: `HostIo`, `host()`, the `Read` and `Write` traits, `read_char`, `write_char`, `write_bytes`, `write` and `writeln`. Use the `Stdout`, `Stderr` and `Stdin` effects with `print!`, `println!` or `StdinReader` instead. A native `main` takes no parameters: `pub fn main(argc: i32, argv: **u8)` and `std::native::Args::new` are gone; declare `uses (args: Args)` from `std::env` instead.
