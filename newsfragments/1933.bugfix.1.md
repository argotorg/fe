A `mut` pattern binding of a packed storage field now writes to the field when the pattern is on a projection's result (`let Small { mut a, .. } = s.me()`, or in a `match`, `if let` or `for`) or is a refutable `if let`, `let else` or `while let`; the writes were silently lost. A projection called after `&&` in a condition now runs the code after its `yield`, which was skipped.
