A trailing `...` makes an `extern` function variadic, so native programs can call C functions such as `fcntl(_ fd: i32, _ cmd: i32, ...) -> i32`. A call passes the fixed parameters, then unlabelled extra arguments, which must be `i32`, `u32`, `i64`, `u64`, `isize`, `usize` or raw pointers. Variadic functions are native-only and can't be generic, take effects or be `const`.
