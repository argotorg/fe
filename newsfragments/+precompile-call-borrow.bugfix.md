Calling a precompile through `std::evm::crypto` (for example `ecrecover`, `sha256` or `modexp`) while a mutable borrow of contract storage is live, such as inside a `mut self` method on a contract field, is no longer rejected as a borrow conflict. A `STATICCALL` is treated as a possible read of contract state through a callback, which a precompile cannot make; `std` now calls precompiles through `ops::staticcall_precompile`, which only accesses its input and output buffers. Static calls to other addresses keep the check.
