Added `std::evm::heap::StorageHeap<T>`, a binary min-heap in contract storage after Solady's `MinHeapLib`, for any `T` that implements `Ord` and `Copy`, including packed structs and multi-word values with their own order. It provides `push`, `pop`, `root`, `push_pop`, `replace` and `enqueue`, which keeps the `max_len` greatest values and reports its outcome as `Enqueued::Added`, `Enqueued::Rejected` or `Enqueued::Replaced(evicted)`. `Reverse<T>` inverts the order, so `StorageHeap<Reverse<T>>` is a max-heap. `pop` and `replace` on an empty heap revert with Solady's `HeapIsEmpty()`.
