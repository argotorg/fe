Add `core::abi::AbiRecordLayout`, the `AbiRecord<N>` trait and the checked prefix sum `abi_record_layout`, which compute the ABI head offsets and head size of a record from its fields. Built-in tuples, generated `msg` variants and `#[error]` structs, and `#[event]` structs, over their data fields, implement `AbiRecord<N>`, and their encoders and decoders read offsets from its `LAYOUT`, so all of them share one definition of ABI head layout.
