Added tuple structs such as `struct Month(pub u8)`, which make newtypes much lighter to write. A tuple struct is constructed by calling its name like a function (`Month(10)`, also generic as in `Wrapper(5)` or `Wrapper<u8>(5)`), its fields are accessed and assigned by position (`m.0`, `m.0 = 11`), and it is destructured with a tuple pattern in `let` and `match` (`let Month(raw) = m`, `Month(2) => 28`). Fields are private unless marked `pub`, and constructing or destructuring a tuple struct outside its module requires all of its fields to be visible. Tuple structs can be generic and have a `where` clause after the field list (`struct Wrapper<T>(pub T) where T: Copy`), and they work as contract storage fields. `#[event]`, `#[error]` and `#[abi]` remain limited to structs with named fields.
