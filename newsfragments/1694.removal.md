`AsBytes::as_bytes()` and `String<N>::as_bytes()` now return `PackedBytes<N>`; `String<N>::from_bytes()` accepts packed bytes. Convert ordinary arrays explicitly with `PackedBytes::from_array()` and `.to_array()`. Custom `AsBytes` implementations now implement `append_to`; the generic `__as_bytes` intrinsic and implicit serialization of arbitrary integers, arrays, enums and structs have been removed.
