Byte spans, `MemBuffer` and `DynString` are ordered as text, and every `MemSlice` of `Ord` elements is ordered element by element. `MemSlice`, `MemArray` and `MemVec` have a `sort` method that sorts elements of any `Ord` type in place by swapping them, so a `MemVec<DynString>` or `MemVec<MemBuffer>` can be sorted; `core::sort::sort` still requires `Copy` elements.
