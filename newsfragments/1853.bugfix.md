At `-O 1` and `-O 2`, a checked operation whose result is unused, such as `let m = b - (a & 255)`, now checks the operand as written. The optimizer dropped the `& 255` and checked `b - a`, which reverted with `Panic(0x11)` for `a = 2000` and `b = 1000`. An `^` operand was dropped the same way, so `2 - (a ^ 5)` with `a = 1` checked `2 - 1` and did not revert.
