Support boolean const `where` predicates on items, such as `fn operation() where allowed(3) {}`, `const fn bounded<const N: usize>() where N > 0` and `struct Bounded<const N: usize> where N > 0`. Conditions without generic parameters are evaluated where they are declared, even if the item is unused. Conditions on generic functions, structs, enums and inherent methods are checked at every use, and a generic caller, including an anonymous constant in its signature or body, satisfies one only by stating the same condition. Const predicates in traits, generic trait impls and on generic `impl` blocks themselves are rejected for now.
