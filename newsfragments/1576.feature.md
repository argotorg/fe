Support boolean const `where` predicates on items, such as `fn operation() where allowed(3) {}` and `const fn bounded<const N: usize>() where N > 0`. Conditions without generic parameters are evaluated where they are declared, even if the item is unused. Conditions on a generic function are checked at every use after inference, and a generic caller, including an anonymous constant in its signature or body, satisfies one only by stating the same condition. Const predicates on generic types, traits, impls and associated functions are rejected for now.
