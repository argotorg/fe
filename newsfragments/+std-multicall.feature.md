Added `std::evm::multicall`, the counterpart of OpenZeppelin's `Multicall`: a contract that handles `MulticallMsg` (`multicall(bytes[])`) with `multicall::multicall(data)` lets callers batch several of its messages into one transaction. Each entry is `DELEGATECALL`ed to the contract itself and the returndata of every entry is returned in order; the first failing entry reverts the batch with its revert data unchanged, or with `FailedCall()` when it reverted without data.
