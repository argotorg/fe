`is_some`, `is_none`, `is_ok`, `is_err` and `unwrap` work on the optional accesses projections return, such as `v.get(i)` and `v.last()`: `v.get(0).unwrap()` reads the element, `v.last().unwrap() = x` writes it, and `node.child(0).unwrap().child(1)` chains. On an empty variant `unwrap` panics as it does on a value.
