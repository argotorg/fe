Writing through a `mut` buffer parameter with `set_byte`, or similar raw byte writes, no longer causes false move or borrow conflicts with unrelated local values, such as a fresh `ByteBuffer` released in a loop or a process argument read after the write. If the written bytes could overwrite the parameter's own pointer, the caller now has to show that they cannot, so passing a fresh buffer is accepted while a buffer whose data points into itself is still rejected at the call.
