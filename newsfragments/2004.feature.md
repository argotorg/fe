`Call::forward_delegatecall(addr, gas)` forwards the current calldata to `addr` with `DELEGATECALL` and returns or reverts with the callee's returndata unchanged, the fallback of a delegating proxy. A proxy built on it spends about 100 to 170 gas a call less than one that copies through `MemBuffer`, `raw_delegatecall` and `copy_returndata`.
