Fixed three native bugs. `core::keccak`, `std::evm::keccak256` and `keccak_words` of data known only at runtime no longer fail to compile. A failed allocation now reverts with `Panic(0x41)` instead of trapping. `std::evm::packed` no longer truncates sizes above 64 bits, which let a `packed_width` of `2**64` write past its allocation.
