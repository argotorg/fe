`MemArray::from_fn(len, |i| ..)` builds an array from a callable, and `MemArray::get(i)` returns a copy of an element, or `None` past the end. `MemSpan::read_word` and `write_word`, and `MemBuffer::read_word`, read and write a 32-byte word at any byte offset, reverting unless the whole word is in range.
