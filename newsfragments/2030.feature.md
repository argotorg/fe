`std::io` adds the `Read` and `Write` traits, `MemReader`, and `BufReader`, which reads a byte or a line at a time (`read_line`, and `read_line_max` with a length limit). `std::fs` adds `File` and `OpenOptions`: `Fs::open`, `create` and `open_with` open a file that reads and writes through those traits and closes when dropped. `io::Error` has new kinds for sockets such as `WouldBlock`, `TimedOut` and `ConnectionRefused`, `Error::new(kind)` makes an error without a host error number, and `Error::code()` now returns an `Option<i32>`.
