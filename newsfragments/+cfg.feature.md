Items and modules can be compiled conditionally with `#[cfg(..)]`, which keeps an item only when its predicate holds: `target = "evm"` or `"native"`, `target_os = "linux"` or `"macos"`, or `target_family = "unix"`. An item with several `cfg` attributes needs all of them to hold, and `#![cfg(..)]` at the top of a module applies to all of its items. Native builds take the operating system from the host; EVM code and SP1 guests have none. `cfg` on fields, variants, associated items, extern functions and `for` loops is an error.
