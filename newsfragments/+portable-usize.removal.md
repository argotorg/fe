`as` casts must be lossless on every target, where `usize` and `isize` take the target's width (32 bits on WebAssembly, 64 on native targets, 256 on EVM): `u8`, `u16` and `u32` convert to `usize`, `usize` to `u256`, and a memory pointer to and from `usize`, but `u64 as usize`, `u256 as usize`, `usize as u32` and `usize as u64` are rejected. Use `downcast()`, `downcast_saturate()` or `downcast_unchecked()` instead. A state pointer such as `StorPtr<T>` still converts to and from its `u256` slot. `core::size_of` returns `usize`, and `core::ptr`'s allocation sizes, copy lengths and offsets (`alloc_bytes`, `offset`, `offset_bytes`, `zero_bytes`, `copy_raw`) are `usize`.
