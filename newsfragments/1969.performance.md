A `match` arm reached by several paths through the match's tests compiles its body once instead of once per path, as with each alternative of a `|` arm or a catch-all `_` arm after a tuple of patterns; each path binds its names and jumps to the body. An arm whose `|` alternatives bind an access at different parts still compiles once per alternative. A contract with a four-alternative arm and a catch-all arm shrinks from 1259 to 743 bytes of runtime code. Checking a pattern no longer doubles in cost for each `|` whose first alternative matches anything, such as `(a, _) | (_, a)`.
