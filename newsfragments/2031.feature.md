`std::net` adds networking for native programs on Linux and macOS. `Ipv4Addr`, `Ipv6Addr`, `IpAddr` and `SocketAddr` parse and print addresses on every target. The `Net` capability connects a `TcpStream`, opens a `TcpListener` or a `UdpSocket`, and resolves host names with `lookup`. `Poller` waits for events on many sockets at once, with `epoll` on Linux and `kqueue` on macOS.
