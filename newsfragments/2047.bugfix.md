A recv arm or `init` that lists a contract effect without `mut`, such as `uses (call)` in `contract C uses (call: mut Call)`, can no longer use it mutably: the arm's own `mut` decides, as it does for contract fields. Such an arm with no other `mut` effect now has a `view` ABI rather than `nonpayable`. Listing an effect `mut` that the contract declares without `mut` is now error 8-0162.
