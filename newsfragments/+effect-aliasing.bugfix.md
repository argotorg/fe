A call's effects now follow the rule its arguments do: two effects of one call may not overlap where one is `mut`, so `with (totals.log, mut totals) { settle() }` is rejected, naming both effects, as is a storage effect beside a capability that reaches all state. In return a function takes its effects to be disjoint, as its parameters are, and may iterate a view of one effect while it writes another. Two effects whose keys one provider may satisfy, such as generic keys that are one type or two trait keys, may still be given one place, which no longer loses writes through a packed storage lane or reads a stale copy. See `docs/language/effects.md`.
