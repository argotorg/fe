On native targets, including SP1, `usize`, `isize`, raw memory pointers and references are 64 bits, the width of the target's addresses; they were 256-bit words, so every index, length and pointer was a four-limb value. A loop counting in `usize` runs about 30 times faster, and a `MemVec` scan about 45 times faster. `MemVec` and `MemBuffer` take 24 bytes and `Box` 8, and a `main(argc: i32, argv: **u8)` receives the host's `argv` without a copy.
