Unchecked arithmetic now applies to `Wei`, `Duration` and `Timestamp`, and to any type that implements the wrapping operator traits (`WrappingAdd`, `WrappingSub`, `WrappingMul`, and the new `WrappingPow` and `WrappingNeg`); operators on other types stay checked. A function's `#[arithmetic(unchecked)]` now also covers the closures and constant expressions in its body.
