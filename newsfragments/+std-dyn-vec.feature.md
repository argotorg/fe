Added `std::abi::DynVec<T>`, a growable builder for ABI arrays of any element type, including dynamic ones such as `Bytes`, nested `DynArray`s and `#[abi]` structs with dynamic fields. Build it with `new()` and `push`, or pre-fill it with `filled(len, value)` and replace elements with `set`; `to_dyn_array()` produces the canonical Solidity encoding, e.g. a `bytes[]` of multicall results.
