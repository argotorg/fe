Collection lengths, indices and offsets are now `usize` instead of `u256`: the lengths, indexing and element and byte offsets of `MemArray`, `MemSlice`, `MemSpan`, `MemBuffer`, `FixedMemBuffer`, `DynArray`, `Bytes`, `DynString`, `BytesView` and `StringView`. Convert with `as u256` where a word is needed. Raw pointer helpers, EVM opcode wrappers and ABI codec positions keep `u256`.
