Added `std::evm::signature` for signature verification: `parse` decodes 65-byte and 64-byte EIP-2098 compact ECDSA signatures into an `EcdsaSig`, `recover` returns the canonical (low `s`) signer as an `Address` and `recover_raw` accepts anything the ECRecover precompile does. `is_valid_erc1271_signature_now` and `is_valid_signature_now` check ERC-1271 contract signatures and ECDSA-or-ERC-1271 signatures with OpenZeppelin `SignatureChecker` semantics.
