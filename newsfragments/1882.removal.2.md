In `#[arithmetic(unchecked)]` code, an overflowing operator on a type parameter or associated type requires its wrapping trait: `a + b` on `T: Add` is now an error, and needs `T: WrappingAdd` to wrap, or `a.add(b)` to stay checked. Where the mode comes from `fe.toml`, such an operator without the bound calls `Add`. It used to wrap when instantiated at an integer, as `x.add(y)` on an integer did in unchecked code; both now panic on overflow.
