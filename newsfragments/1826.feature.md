Solidity value types now pack into shared storage slots inside structs, tuples and map entries, like `bool` and the narrow integers already do: `Address` takes 20 bytes, `FixedBytes<N>` takes `N`, the odd-width integers such as `Uint24` take their width, and any newtype (a struct with a single field) packs like its field, or at its `SolPacked` width when it has one. `struct Rec { owner: Address, flag: bool, stamp: u64 }` now takes one slot, as in Solidity. Writing a value its bytes cannot hold, such as a `FixedBytes<4>` whose `val` has more than four bytes, reverts.
