`SolSlot`, `StorageMap`, `StoragePackedArray`, `StorageHeap`, `SolArray`, `SolEnumerableSet` and `StorageBytes` can be opened at a raw storage slot, such as a Solidity contract's known layout: `unsafe { StorageMap<K, V>::at(slot) }` gives the collection to read, with shared `RawStorage` authority, and `at_mut(slot)` gives it to read and write, with `mut RawStorage`.
