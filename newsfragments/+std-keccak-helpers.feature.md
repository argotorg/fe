Added `std::evm::keccak_words([..])` for hashing a fixed list of words and `std::evm::create2_address(deployer, salt, init_code_hash)`. `keccak_packed` and `encode_packed` now write each value directly at its packed width instead of going through the `Packed` builder, which makes them about four times cheaper, and custom-width Solidity ints (`sol::Int24` etc.) now implement `EncodePacked`.
