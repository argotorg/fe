Added `get_checked` and `set_checked` to `StoragePackedArray`. The existing `get` and `set` are deliberately unbounded: indices map arithmetically onto packed slot words, so an index past the caller's modeled domain silently spills into the next region of storage instead of failing — for example a 16-cell board backed by `StoragePackedArray<4>` accepts `set(16, v)` and writes a lane into bits 64-67 of the second slot word. The new accessors take a const-generic modeled length and revert with Solidity's standard `Panic(uint256)` payload (code 0x32, `IndexOutOfBounds`) when the index is out of domain, matching the signal the compiler already emits for failed fixed-array bounds checks.
