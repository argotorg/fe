Added `std::evm::erc4337` for ERC-4337 accounts and paymasters on EntryPoint v0.7 and v0.8: the `PackedUserOperation` ABI struct with unpacking of its gas limits, fees and paymaster fields and its v0.7 and v0.8 user operation hashes (`hash_v07`, `hash_v08`), `AccountMsg` (`validateUserOp`) and `PaymasterMsg` (`validatePaymasterUserOp`, `postOp`) with `PostOpMode`, `ValidationData` packing and unpacking with `SIG_VALIDATION_SUCCESS` and `SIG_VALIDATION_FAILED`, `require_entry_point`, `pay_prefund`, and the canonical `ENTRY_POINT_V07` and `ENTRY_POINT_V08` addresses. `validate_user_op` implements an account's `validateUserOp` from a `UserOpSigner` signature check, like OpenZeppelin's `Account`: it rejects callers other than the EntryPoint, checks the signature, pays the prefund and returns the validation data.
