On WebAssembly, a struct or tuple of at most five fields in all, each an integer of at most 64 bits, a `bool` or a pointer, is now held in values rather than in its function's frame, as on native targets. A call in tail position that passes one is a tail call that reuses its caller's frame, so an interpreter whose handlers pass a cursor to each other in tail position, such as fevm, runs in constant stack instead of overflowing it.
