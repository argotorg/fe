`std::time` gives native programs on Linux and macOS the host's clocks through the sealed capability `Clock`, which `main` and tests take from `std::native::Host` with `uses (clock: Clock)`. `instant()` reads the monotonic clock as an `Instant`, and `now()` the wall clock as the `Duration` since the Unix epoch. A `Duration` counts nanoseconds in a `u64`, with `from_secs`, `from_millis`, `from_micros` and `from_nanos`, the matching `as_*` methods, `+`, `-` and comparisons; `Instant::duration_since` gives the time between two readings, and `instant + duration` a later one.
