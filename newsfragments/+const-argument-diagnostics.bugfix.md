Const arguments and bounds that were silently dropped or misreported are now checked where they are written: faulting or non-const const arguments in trait references, including those of a qualified path's trait, super-traits that fail to resolve, associated-type bindings and bounds, effect-key arguments, and non-const calls in generic constants. Unresolved or invalid types in a trait reference's arguments, including nested ones, are reported at the argument instead of dropping the bound. A fault that only an applied default or an evaluated `const fn` reveals is reported at that use, pointing to the faulting computation. Associated-type bindings in `impl` headers are now rejected.
