`fe build --backend wasm` now builds ingots and workspaces as well as single files. An ingot's exports are the non-generic `pub fn`s of its root module, `src/lib.fe`, and they may call its other modules and its dependencies. A workspace build writes one module per member with exports, and `--ingot <name>` selects one member. `--wasm-stack-size <bytes>` sets the stack size, 1 MiB by default. Released `fe` binaries now include the WebAssembly backend.
