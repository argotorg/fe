`fe build --backend wasm` now exports the functions marked `#[unsafe(export)]` anywhere in the ingot, as a native library does, instead of every non-generic `pub fn` in the root module: mark the functions a module should export. Two exports with one name are an error. A WebAssembly export may take the effects of `std::wasm::Host`, so a function that does cannot also be exported from a native library.
