Outside a block `with (Provider) { .. }`, which chooses a provider on purpose, items that a provider generates in the package that asked for them only fill gaps: a name, a member or a method that the package's own code or a dependency answers keeps its answer, and a generated item that nothing could reach is an error at its request. The traits whose presence the compiler consults to choose behaviour declare `#[decides = "..."]` in `core` and `std`. A generated implementation of one of them, or of a trait that a bound of a written implementation of one names, is refused at its request. A header `impl Trait for Type with (Provider)` is the way to ask a provider for such an implementation, and it may name a trait of `core`, `std` or another package, including a trait with parameters besides its target type. The target may be a struct that `#[abi]`, `#[error]` or `#[event]` expands or a `msg` variant, a marker trait takes an empty provider output, and a callable trait such as `Fn(A) -> R` takes the call method that its provider writes for those parameters. See `docs/language/generation.md`.
